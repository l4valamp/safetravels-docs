# Demo Golden Path

This serves as the "Golden" timeline for a first time players run through the demo. 

I a player plays optimally they will experience everything "beat" here in the designated amount of time. 

All edge cases and failures should accounted for so that every beat here is experienced. 

| TIME       | METHOD                      | DESCRIPTION                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------- | --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0 seconds  | Cutscene / Gameplay         | Open on a cab driving on the road, the player is<br>suddenly given control. Text on screen explains how<br>to drive, turn, slow down, etc.<br><br>At this point it is currently day time and the map is well lit.                                                                                                                                                 |
| 20 seconds | Cutscene                    | The player enters a tunnel, then exits at a tunnel<br>near the “lookout point”.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 30 seconds | Gameplay                    | Camera pans and zooms to draw attention to the town<br>below. The player's phone rings and directs them to their<br>first passenger. The gameplay loop has begun.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 2 mins     | Gameplay/Passenger Dialogue | The player is introduced to the tourist, who introduces<br>them to dialogue systems and reinforces driving now in a<br>city. This drive is notably uneventful. The drive ends with<br>them gaining the map.                                                                                                                                                       |
| 7 mins     | Gameplay                    | Drop off the tourist, then get a call to your next location<br>and passenger, the ____.<br><br>**At this point the day has shifted to dusk.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 9 min      | Gameplay/Passenger Dialogue | Another uneventful trip with one of the towns locals that delivers more insight into the town and recent events before your arrival.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 12 min     | Gameplay                    | Drop off the tourist, then get a call to your next location<br>and passenger, the anomaly.<br><br>**At this the day has shifted to night**                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 14 min     | Gameplay/Passenger Dialogue | The player is introduced to the anomaly. They give<br>insights into the idea that anomalous things are<br>happening within the town. They must juggle a mechanic<br>where their passenger tries to kill them if they fall asleep.<br>The drive ends with a promise for the anomaly to share<br>their findings about the anomalies in the town with the<br>player. |
| 18 min     | Gameplay                    | The player gets a call for their next passenger, the<br>dockworker, shortly after driving off they are pursued by<br>an angry car that tries to drive them off the road.<br>Successfully arriving at the docks will show the pursuer<br>accidentally driving into the ocean. At this point the<br>dockworker gets in the car.                                     |
| 20 min     | Gameplay/Passenger Dialogue | The player is introduced to the dockworker, who is<br>largely uneventful but provides insights into the opinions<br>of the towns people about the hotel. The dockworker<br>thinks it is great for the economy and people are being<br>whiny. This drive is brief.                                                                                                 |
| 24 min     | Gameplay                    | As soon as the dockworker gets out of the car, a strange<br>man with a bomb strapped to his chest gets in. He<br>instructs you to take him to the same location you<br>dropped the anomaly off at. If the bomb timer ticks to 0<br>your entire car explodes and you must start this<br>sequence over.                                                             |
| 25 min     | Gameplay/Passenger Dialogue | The man in a hurry gets out of the car, runs into the<br>location, and the whole location explodes, launching your<br>car back and severely damaging it                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 27 min     | Gameplay                    | The player drives further down the road on rickety<br>wheels. They see the only thing forward is an autobody<br>shop. Upon pulling into the garage for repairs, the demo<br>ends                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 28 min     | Cutscene                    | “Thank you for playing” screen. End of demo.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
