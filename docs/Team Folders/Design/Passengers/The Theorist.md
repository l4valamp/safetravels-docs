| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The Theorist                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Appearance**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| A Tired Middle Aged Man<br>![[Pasted image 20261112224553.png\|258]]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Premise**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| -An outsider whos been staying in the town in order to research the hotel after hearing reports about its sudden arrival and the subsequent oddities that have begun popping up around town. He's seems very tired and deliurious assumedly form late night of studying. When ever you aren't under a streetlamp he begins to fall asleep. When fully asleep he will become "entranced" and will attack the player unless they drive under a streetlamp in order to wake him up. |
| **Thematic Argument**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -Is finding answers really worth if its to the people around you and your own detriment?<br><br>-                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Surface Level Impressions**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| A researcher who is fascinated by the happenings of the hotel. When speaking about the hotel he seems more enamored by the hotel as opposed to horrified.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Deeper Truths**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| The Hotel has consumed him and he has essentially become another pawn to the hotels command.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Point of Discovery**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| When ever your in the dark he falls asleep and is entranced by the hotel.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Application of theme in the mechanics**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Gameplay mechanic where whenever your car is not in a street lamps light he begins to fall asleep. Routes will have specific lamp posts lit that the player can folow to survive.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
