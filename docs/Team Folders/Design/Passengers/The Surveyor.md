| **Premise**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| the surveyor wants you to take her to several points around the woods so she can step out of the cab for a minute and count the trees. Along the way, the player can expose her to something supernatural, which rattles her. Despite this, she counts the trees in the area and moves on. She does not record the (obviously more significant) supernatural element in her final report, since there’s not a section for it on her paperwork. |
| **Thematic Argument**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Sometimes facts and figures miss the big picture. Are we measuring the right things? Literally “missing the forest for the trees.”                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Surface Level Impressions**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| The surveyor is a mature and competent government worker that knows what she’s doing and wants to get to the bottom of what’s causing the rot in the woods. She's here to be the adult in the room and make sense of strange phenomena with good old fashioned data collection.                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Deeper Truths**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Even in the face of something that defies everything she thought she knew about forestry and life itself, she clings to methodology and standards that are woefully unequipped for the world around her. These tools of measurement let her feel in control, but also bury glaring problems. Her tree density survey will be the only government record of the strange ongoings in this town.                                                  |
| **Point of Discovery**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| - choosing to take her to a visible (or audible?) environmental anomaly in the woods.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Application of theme in the mechanics**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| - if the anomaly is just an auditory thing (heartbeat, intense humming from the trees), she takes pictures at every spot you bring her, which the player can keep. The picture she takes of the anomaly spot won’t capture the full story of the weirdness going on there.<br>- more freedom of choice of where to take her (as long as the stops are in the woods)                                                                            |
