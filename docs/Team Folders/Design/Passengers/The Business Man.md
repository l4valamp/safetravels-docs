| **Premise**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| you drive this man several times (twice?) The first time, he’s sweaty and nervous for a meeting and rushes you. If you make it in time, he’s very grateful. If you make him late, he is very upset and frightened. The second time he gets in the cab, he’s even more frantic rushing you. His briefcase is ticking. If you fail to deliver him to his destination in time, the briefcase explodes and kills you both. If you succeed, he runs out of the cab and suicide-bombs something.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Thematic Argument**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| - people in power are at risk of losing their power if they don’t satisfy the whims of the political machine. Maybe showing how much power the hotel has over the people we think of as being “at the top”.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Surface Level Impressions**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| - A terse and no-nonsense businessman                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Deeper Truths**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| - a frantic man terrified of the hotel and the power it holds over him and his money.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Point of Discovery**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| - slowing down on his route pushes him to blurt more about his meeting and why it’s important to him/how it’s shady. He is frightened, pleading, and desperate, as opposed to the politician's anger.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Application of theme in the mechanics**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Countdown timer to get to the destination lest the bomb goes off, encouraging the player to take illegal and off-road shortcuts.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Points of Uncertainty**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| - where he’s going and what he’s bombing. Maybe his first destination is a meeting with the hotel?<br>- An important ribbon cutting? Maybe he’s a politician? If you’re late, he loses the election, and the next time you see him he has nothing to lose and is acting out against the hotel.<br>- Maybe he’s going to a meeting to join the hotel. He fears retribution if he misses it. The second time you see him, he’s horrified by what he’s learned. The thing he’s bombing is seemingly innocuous, Maybe the explosion reveals something hidden.<br>- I think I like some of these ideas explored more in the politician. I think he should instead be panicked and frightened instead of angry and status-obsessed. |
| **Potential strengths in the idea**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| - I like the mechanic of being in a rush and the ticking time bomb briefcase.<br>- I like someone seeming composed but getting more frantic the longer you take<br>- I like the need to take illegal or offroad shortcuts to get to a destination in time                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
