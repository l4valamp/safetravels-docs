# The Politician

| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| **Appearance**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ![[Screenshot 2026-10-27 230753.png]]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Premise**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| A test character to implement "Character reacting to your driving" passenger logic. The Politician wants to keep up his smiling, polished image, but every little thing you do just gets to him! He could remain completely charming throughout your trip, but if you keep testing him, he gradually falls into a blind rage and threatens to kill you if you don't do exactly what he says. He feels like he's losing control of the town, and is therefore a control freak to you.                                                                                                                          |
| **Thematic Argument**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| He is lying to himself and others about how much control he has over the situation with the hotel. This is putting a band-aid on an urgent situation, and preventing real preventative action from happening. It is also taking a toll on his mental health (lol).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Surface Level Impressions**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| An almost robotically sanitized, beloved local politician. He assures you that though people have been nervous about the hotel recently, he has everything under control.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Deeper Truths**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| A man on his last thread, broiling with rage and desperation. He feels like he has lost all of his power to the hotel's steamrolling and ignoring. Being ignored and disrespected by a reckless cab driver is his last straw.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Point of Discovery**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| The first time you mess up he tells you not to do that again. His haughty bragging about having everything under control motivates the player to frazzle him. He gets more and more frazzled the more errors the player makes while driving.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Application of theme in the mechanics**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| The Politician has a mood scale that gradually ticks down the more the player disrespects him. (Time spent off-road, in the wrong lane, hitting something, taking the wrong turns, stopping the car.) His language shifts throughout. As he becomes more frazzled he reveals more details about his dealing with the hotel (Vaguely). In his final enraged state, he pulls a gun or knife on the player, and threatens to kill them if they don't do exactly what he says. As soon as the player is following his rules again, he goes back to normal. When he's dropped off, he thanks you and leaves a tip. |
| **Points of Uncertainty**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| None this guy is goated.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Potential strengths in the idea**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| - This character should be so haughty and self assured and sanitized so that he is maximally fun to tick off. <br>- As reactive as possible to all of the player's poor driving.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
