# The Sap

| **Name**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The Sap                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Appearance**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ![[Pasted image 20261027201705.png]]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Premise**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Older woman who has a lot of love for the town and stories to tell. Everywhere you go reminds her of another story. Remembers the town as how it used to be. Refuses to see that its gone downhill. This is a test character to implement "Character-Reacting-to-world" logic on passengers. |
| **Thematic Argument**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Don't retreat into nostalgia to avoid facing harsh current realities.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Surface Level Impressions**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Kindly and talkative old lady with sweet and nostalgic hijinks stories and insight into people and places in the city and surrounding towns.                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Deeper Truths**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| The Sap refuses to acknowledge that things have changed in the city recently. She has immense cognitive dissonance regarding how she thinks about the present state of the town and the reality around you.                                                                                  |
| **Point of Discovery**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| When you finally drop her off to her "home", a burned down husk of a cottage, and it becomes glaringly apparent how delusional she is.                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Application of theme in the mechanics**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| The Sap is hyper-responsive to the player's current location and landmarks you pass by. The Sap may not always describe landmarks accurately, guiding players to realize the passengers are unreliable narrators and notice clues in their environment.                                      |
| **Points of Uncertainty**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Specific landmarks and points of dissonance that would be filled in as the map gets fleshed out.<br>Where is she coming from and where is she going?                                                                                                                                         |
| **Potential strengths in the idea**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| - I like that this fleshes out mechanics related to hyper-reactivity to the environment.<br>- I also like that this will require implementing longer, trailing series of dialogue boxes.                                                                                                     |
