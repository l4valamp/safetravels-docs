# The Three Amigas


| **Name**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The Three Amigas                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Appearance**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ![[Screenshot 2026-10-27 234641.png]]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Premise**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| three best friends get in your cab after a night partying. You can drop them off in any order, and they don’t give much instruction while driving. Instead, they gossip with each other. When they’re all together, they’re good friends, but after you drop the first person off, the other two will always start gossiping about that person, revealing secrets they’re keeping from them. |
| **Thematic Argument**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ignoring obvious issues for the sake of comfort/keeping the peace/getting along just puts a band aid on obviously unhealthy circumstances.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Surface Level Impressions**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| - these three friends are lots of fun and love each other to bits!<br>- (Golden girls, heathers, powerpuff girls)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Deeper Truths**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| - each person is deeply insecure about their place in the group. They each resent the other two.<br>- Some kind of cheating secret<br>- Lack of invitation to a girls vacation<br>- A bold choice one of them is making that the other two are encouraging because they think it is a huge mistake.                                                                                          |
| **Point of Discovery**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| - after dropping off the first passenger, the other two will turn their conversation viciously against them.<br>- After dropping off the second passenger, the last one will let their guard down completely and reveal their true self (crying while venting to you, calling someone on the phone, going quiet and stone-faced?)                                                            |
| **Application of theme in the mechanics**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| - we see how great of a time everyone can have if they keep their heads in the ground about the truth.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Points of Uncertainty**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| - how can their interpersonal dramas tie a little more into the state of the town?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Potential strengths in the idea**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| - I think juicy messy gossip is fun as a general rule<br>- I like how 1-1 they mirror the “turning a blind eye” theme of the town<br>- I like the player autonomy of deciding who to drop off and thus to learn more about. I also think it’s the kind of thing people like to read about in wikis or through videos.<br>- Fun mix of fun and depressing/grim                                |
